## Posting checklist

1. Post the main tweet with **01-announcement.png** attached. Add the alt text.
2. Reply with tweets 2–5 in order, attaching **03-selected-work.png** to 4 and **04-proof.png** to 5. Attach **02-what-i-build.png** to tweet 3 if you want every tweet to carry an image.
3. Quote-post the thread from your own account the next day with the video, or post the video separately — X gives video its own distribution.
4. Pin the thread to your profile.
5. Hashtags: one or two at most, on the final tweet only. X's own guidance is that hashtags beyond that reduce engagement, so `#SpeechAI #AfricanLanguages` is the ceiling — do not add more.

Do not put the link in the first tweet if you are optimising purely for reach; X down-ranks posts with outbound links. The thread above keeps the link in the last tweet, which gets you both: reach on the hook, traffic at the end.

To edit copy, change the text in `assets/cards.html` or `assets/video.html`, then run:

```text
node media/twitter/assets/capture-cards.mjs
node media/twitter/assets/capture-video.mjs
node media/twitter/assets/capture-poster.mjs 14.2
```

Stills are 16:9 because that is how X renders a single image in the timeline; a 1:1 crop would leave dead space on both sides. Keep the safe area clear: X overlays the alt badge in the bottom-right corner of the image.
