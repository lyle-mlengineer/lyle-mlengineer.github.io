How it works

From brief to broadcast

  1. 01

    Describe the voice

    Pick a starting point — warm, dry, authoritative, bright — or clone a consented sample. Name it, version it, keep it.

  2. 02

    Direct the read

    Highlight a line, nudge the emphasis, slow the final clause. Hear the change before the sentence ends.

  3. 03

    Ship it anywhere

    Export broadcast-ready WAV, or stream through the API into your app in 42 milliseconds.
