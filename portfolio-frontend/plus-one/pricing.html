    03 — Fine print

    The awkward
    questions.

    If something is not covered here, the studio inbox answers within a day.

    What happens when I cancel?

    Your albums stay readable, and every frame you already downloaded keeps working. New rolls pause at the end of the paid period — nothing is deleted.

    Can I switch between monthly and annual?

    Yes. Annual is billed once for twelve months with two months free, and switching takes effect on your next billing date. The difference is prorated in your favour.

    Who can see the photos and the faces?

    Only the people you invite to a roll. Faces are stored encrypted, used solely to render your own albums, and never used to train anything shared.

    Do I own the frames?

    Completely. Download them, print them, put them on a birthday card. Plus One never resells or licenses your album, and your images are excluded from any public gallery unless you publish one.

    Is this a real payment?

    No. This checkout is a working demo: it validates the form, applies invite codes and issues a receipt, but no card is ever charged and no card details leave the page.
