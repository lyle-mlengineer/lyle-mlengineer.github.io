01 — How it works

Three things in.
A whole day out.

Plus One isn't a filter and it isn't a photo editor. You hand it the people, the place and the plan — it keeps producing the frames nobody had a free hand to take.

See an album
  1. 01

    Drop the faces

    Four to eight photos per person. Phone shots, old shots, bad shots — it needs to learn a face, not a pose.

  2. 02

    Write the brief

    The venue, the date, who's coming, and the activities you'd hate to miss. Plain language is enough.

    03 — Why it holds up

    It has to look like a real day.
    Mostly it has to look like yours.

    05 — Fine print

    Reasonable questions.

    Whose face can I add?

    Only people who have said yes. Every person you add confirms their own invite, and can pull their face out of any album at any time.

    What if it gets someone wrong?

    Flag the frame. It retrains on your correction before the next event, and a discarded frame is never used to generate anything again.

    Where do the photos live?

    In a private album only your guest list can open. Nothing is used to train shared models, and you can export or delete everything in one click.

    What about venues it has never seen?

    It works from the address, the date, the time and the weather. If the light is wrong, you'll see it straight away — that's the tell we work hardest to remove.
