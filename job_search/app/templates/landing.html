Skipper

My job-search agent

Applying well is not typing. It is re-deciding the same eleven things for every role: what this job really is, who this company is, which projects map to it, and what they will ask me. Skipper runs that pipeline once per posting, from a link or a paste, and leaves me with a pack I can send — not a chat window I have to prompt.

Reads the job

A careers page, a job-board link or pasted text. Skipper fetches the posting, cleans it and pulls out the title, stack, salary, must-haves, nice-to-haves and the red flags worth knowing.

ExtractionSummaryFit gaps

Writes the pack

A tailored CV in PDF and DOCX, a cover letter, the email to send, three spoken answers, a company brief and two interview briefs — each grounded in the portfolio, never in guesswork.

CV · PDF + DOCXCover letterInterview prep

Keeps the board

Every job lives on one dashboard from discovered to offer, with the artifacts attached, the status honest and the next action written down before I forget it.
